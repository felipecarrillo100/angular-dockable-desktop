# M6 — Drag and dock · **PASS**

**Gate:** `npm run gate -- M6` (standing gate, M6 rules, browser gate). 382/382 unit tests.

## What exists

- **`DragDock`** (one per `<ndd-desktop>`): the drag state machine, ported from vdd's
  `useDragDock` onto signals. It has two deliberately different input paths:
  - **mouse/pen:** window listeners with **no** pointer capture, so the drop zones keep their
    hover events, and a 5 px threshold separates a drag from a click;
  - **touch:** a 300 ms long press **with** capture, cancelled by more than 8 px of travel,
    and resolved by hit-testing.

  Drop resolution order is fixed: edge → tab → leaf cross → corner → free float.
  Pointer-move listeners are registered outside Angular's zone (ADR 0006), which M6 rule 1
  checks.
- **`ndd-drop-zones`** (a cross per leaf, with a preview of the split share),
  **`ndd-edge-zones`** (4 edges and 4 corners, where a corner disarms its edge), and
  **`ndd-drag-ghost`** (tab drags only).
- **Tabs:** drag start, left/right insertion markers, and the index correction for reorders
  within a leaf.
- **Floating windows:** a title-bar drag registers with the machine, hit-tests the zones under
  the pointer (the window turns off its own pointer events), and docks on release. Released over
  nothing, the window stays where it was dragged.

## Browser gate (real Chrome, zoneless **and** zone.js)

In LTR and RTL, a tab is dragged with real pointer events to:
- a leaf's centre (it joins the group and becomes active);
- a leaf's bottom (a new leaf in a vertical branch);
- the left workspace edge (the first child under LTR, the **second** under RTL);
- the bottom-right corner (the anchor is `bottom-right` under LTR and **`bottom-left`** under
  RTL, because it is logical);
- nowhere (a free float);
- a reorder onto the right half of a sibling tab ("drag" lands right after "third", and the tab
  count is unchanged).

On an emulated touch device, a tap does not drag, while a real 420 ms press via CDP arms
`ndd-long-press-active`, shows the drop zones, and lands in the target group.

## Differential gate: vdd is the oracle

`scripts/gates/browser/m6-diff.mjs` drives the **same gesture script**
(`lib/dock-gestures.mjs`, one adapter per library) against vdd's playground and ndd's, in the
same viewport, and requires `saveLayout()` to be **equal** after renaming generated leaf ids by
order of appearance.

- **Result:** all 12 sequences (6 cases × 2 directions) are identical to vdd.
- **The vdd playground** is built by the M6 rules from the read-only `vue-dockable-desktop`
  checkout into `artifacts/vdd-playground`. The rules assert `git status` in that checkout is
  unchanged by the build.
- **Negative control** (`--control`, required to fail): a planted direction-mirroring bug on the
  ndd side. It is caught in 4 sequences (edge left and corner, both directions), with the exact
  differing fields reported, for example `.floating.0.anchor: vdd="bottom-right"
  ndd="bottom-left"`.
