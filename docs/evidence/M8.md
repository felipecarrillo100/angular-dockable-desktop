# M8 — Context menus · **PASS**

**Gate:** `npm run gate -- M8` (standing gate, M8 rules, browser gate). 440/440 unit tests.

## What exists

- **`<ndd-context-menu>`** renders whatever menu the workspace has pending, so
  `workspace.showContextMenu()` works from a service too. It supports items, separators,
  one-level submenus (150 ms open delay, 200 ms close grace), checkbox items, disabled items,
  i18n labels and `cyAction` test hooks. It is rendered into `document.body` (`[nddPortal]`) and
  pulled back inside the viewport once measured.
  - **Dismissal** listens to capture-phase `pointerdown` *and* a bubbled `click`, since WebGL
    canvases commonly stop pointerdown; Escape also dismisses.
  - **Custom rendering:** `<ng-template nddContextMenuTemplate let-items let-x="x" let-y="y"
    let-close="close">` replaces the built-in menu (rdd's `ContextMenuAdapter`, vdd's slot). Its
    context is **typed** through `ngTemplateContextGuard`, and strict templates enforced that in
    the spec itself: a separator has no `label`.
  - **WAI-ARIA menu pattern (N3):**
    - focus moves in on open;
    - Arrow keys, Home and End rove across enabled items (skipping separators and disabled
      items);
    - ArrowRight / ArrowLeft (mirrored under RTL) enter and leave a submenu;
    - Escape returns focus to where it was;
    - checkbox items carry `menuitemcheckbox`.
- **Standard menus** (`core/panel-menu.ts`, verbatim from vdd, plus `desktop/menus.ts`), the same
  wherever a panel is right-clicked:
  - a tab offers float, minimise and close, as allowed; items a panel opted out of are absent,
    and an empty menu does not open;
  - a floating window's title bar offers the same, plus a **more-actions** button when the panel
    contributed items;
  - a taskbar icon offers restore, maximise and close, where **Maximize works (D1)**; the
    `taskbarContextMenu` output still fires after it.

  A touch long press that never became a drag opens the tab menu.
- **APIs:**
  - `injectContextMenu()` → an open function;
  - `injectPanelContextMenu(() => items)` → items appended to the calling panel's own menu, read
    fresh on every open and disposed with the component;
  - `[nddContextMenu]="items"` directive.

## Browser gate (real Chrome, zoneless **and** zone.js)

- **Tab menu:** a real right-click opens the standard menu at the pointer.
- **Dismissal:** a real outside click closes it.
- **Clamp:** a menu opened 5 px from the viewport's corner stays fully inside.
- **Keyboard only:** focus lands on the first item; ArrowDown then Enter runs the *second*
  item's action and closes; focus returns to the element focused before; Escape does the same.
- **D1:** a real right-click on a taskbar icon, then a real click on Maximize, leaves the panel
  floating and maximised, and the taskbar empty.
- **Submenu side:** right of the menu under LTR, left of it under RTL.
- **N2:** a hover preview never covers its own icon while animating in (the preview's bottom
  edge is sampled every 15 ms).

**Negative control** (`m8.mjs --control`, run by M8's rules): restore vdd's tooltip keyframes.
It is rejected: "the preview overlaps its icon while animating in (bottom 763 > icon top 753)".

## Findings

1. **N2, a real defect inherited from rdd/vdd, found by this gate.** The zone-build run failed
   D1 because the right-click landed on the *hover preview* instead of the icon. Measuring the
   preview over time showed why: its entry animation starts `translateY(-90%)`, 10% of its own
   height (~18 px) lower than its resting place, which is more than the 8 px gap above the
   icon. For the first frames it covers its icon, so a click or right-click just after hovering
   hits the preview. The slide-in now starts 6 px low, inside the gap. Recorded in
   `docs/PARITY.md` as N2, with the control above pinning it.
2. **Submenu placement overlaps the menu by 2–3 px**, because it is positioned against the
   parent *item*, which sits inside the menu's padding. That is vdd's geometry and is not a
   defect; the gate asserts the side.

## Non-vacuity

Red when stubbed: panel-menu, context-menu, inject-context-menu, menus, core context-menu.
3. **The browser gate's first placement check raced the menu's entry animation.** The menu
   scales in from 0.97 over 100 ms, and `getBoundingClientRect()` includes transforms, so a
   rect read mid-animation sits a few px off the menu's resting place. The zone build hit that
   once ("did not open at the pointer (left 57.3)"). The gate now waits for the element's
   animations to finish before measuring placement, clamp and submenu side. The 2 px tolerance
   is unchanged; the N2 overlap probe still samples *during* the animation, which is its point.
